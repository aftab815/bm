About the Chairman

Syed Shabi ul Hassan Bukhari is the Chairman of BM Solicitor (Private) Limited. With a distinguished career spanning multiple decades, he has led strategic legal advisory and institutional development initiatives across the region. His expertise includes corporate governance, project management, and high-value dispute resolution.

Throughout his tenure, he has worked with corporate clients, government entities, and NGOs to deliver pragmatic legal solutions informed by deep sector knowledge. His leadership emphasizes integrity, client-centric delivery, and capacity building.
